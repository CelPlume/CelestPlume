`BilibiliVideo` and `YouTubeVideo` embed videos as a zero-request facade —
placeholder stage, play button, caption — with **no requests and no iframe**
at build time. The player loads after an explicit click, or when the facade
approaches the viewport with `preload="auto"`.

## Props

Shared props: `title` labels the caption and the iframe for accessibility;
`preload` controls lazy loading — `none` keeps the facade static until
clicked, `auto` initializes the player 240px before the facade enters the
viewport (IntersectionObserver, one-shot).

## Notes

- The stage keeps a strict 16:9 ratio and never breaks the article layout.
- Player URLs are created only in the browser after activation:
  `player.bilibili.com` (high quality, danmaku off) and
  `youtube-nocookie.com` (rel 0, modest branding).
- The caption links to the watch page (Bilibili / YouTube) and opens in a new
  tab.
- Invalid props fail the build with a clear error instead of shipping a broken
  embed.
- For a link card with cover, title, description and uploader, use
  [BilibiliCard](/contribution/components/cards/) instead of the
  playable facade.
