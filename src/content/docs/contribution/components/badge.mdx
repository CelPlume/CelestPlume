A badge labels a short piece of content. Six variants share the semantic
color tokens with callouts.

## All variants

## Props

| Option | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `text` | `string` | — | Badge label (first argument). |
| `variant` | `'default' \| 'gold' \| 'info' \| 'warning' \| 'error' \| 'success'` | `'default'` | Semantic variant. |

## Notes

- Variants map to `--cpd-*` semantic tokens; `gold` keeps the brand accent
  for special highlights.
- Renders as `<span class="cpd-badge cpd-badge-<variant>">`.
